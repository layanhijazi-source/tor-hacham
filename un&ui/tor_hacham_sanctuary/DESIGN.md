## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an ultra-luxurious, calming, and clinically pristine atmosphere tailored for premier aesthetic medicine, dermatology, and high-end beauty spas. It balances the sterile reassurance of medical expertise with the serene indulgence of a five-star wellness retreat.

### Aesthetic Movement: Soft-Touch Neo-Luxe
The visual identity fuses refined minimalism with tactile glassmorphism. Interfaces feel airy, luminous, and weightless, using subtle frosted finishes, featherlight translucent borders, and soothing organic curves. Tactile interactions evoke soft velvet and chilled marble, making digital scheduling and treatment exploration feel like a personal concierge experience.

### Emotional Response
- **Serenity & Trust:** Soothing pastel undertones lower anxiety associated with clinical treatments.
- **Prestige & Indulgence:** Editorial typographic scales and meticulous micro-spacings communicate artisanal care and elite aesthetic standards.
- **Effortless Clarity:** Generous whitespace, razor-sharp contrast on copy, and clean glass surfaces guide the patient seamlessly from discovery to booking.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from botanical skincare, crushed minerals, and calming wellness botanicals. Every shade serves a functional role while preserving a delicate, restorative ambience.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#FFB2B7` - Velvet Coral Rose):** Acts as the signature brand hue. Applied to focal touchpoints, hero badges, confirmed reservation states, and primary interactive highlights.
- **Secondary (`#AAF1D4` - Fresh Mint Sage):** Delivers a crisp, balancing breath of clinical cleanliness and botanical freshness. Used for positive health confirmations, slot availability, and energetic micro-accents.
- **Tertiary / Base Canvas (`#FFECEC` - Rose Milk Surface):** Serves as the warm, luminous background canvas. Replaces sterile cool whites with a nourishing, milky complexion that prevents visual fatigue.
- **Deep Contrast Neutral (`#3A1E24` - Rich Plum Espresso):** Provides commanding typographical hierarchy and optical grounding. Replaces harsh true blacks with a warm, deep plum-infused espresso that achieves AAA accessibility over rose and mint tones.

### Color Distribution Strategy
- **65% Canvas & Tint Tiers:** `#FFECEC` layered with semi-transparent frosted cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` and `rgba(255, 236, 236, 0.85)`).
- **25% Structural Typography & Framing:** `#3A1E24` for deep, legible headers, body text, and structural outlines.
- **10% Radiant Highlights:** Dual-accent pairing of `#FFB2B7` and `#AAF1D4` for primary CTAs, active status indicators, and luxury iconography.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes **Rubik** across all tiers, providing seamless bi-directional harmony across Hebrew and Latin scripts. Its geometric curves with soft terminal transitions directly mirror the system's rounded UI architecture.

### Editorial Nuance & Optical Calibration
- **Headlines:** Display weights maintain a tight tracking (-0.02em to -0.01em) and clean medium-to-semibold weights, delivering refined clinical prestige without aggressive weight.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy leverages deliberate line height ratios (1.55 to 1.62) in `#3A1E24` to ensure total legibility on both soft rose milky backdrops and frosted glass components.
- **Bilingual & RTL Alignment:** Line heights are calibrated higher than typical Western standards to accommodate Hebrew diacritics and ascender/descender heights gracefully.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a relaxed fluid-grid rhythm, echoing the spaciousness of high-end private suites.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Content max-width constrained to `1280px` for optimal eye tracking.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Interactive cards expand edge-to-edge with inset safe padding.

### Rhythm & Padding
Whitespace is treated as an active luxury material. Components breathe through generous padding scales (`space-lg` to `space-xl` internally on modal cards), reinforcing unhurried decision-making throughout booking flows.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through frosted glass strata and warm ambient light scattering rather than heavy physical drops.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Base Surface:** Flat tone `#FFECEC` (Rose Milk).
2. **Elevated Frosted Glass (Cards & Containers):**
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`
   - Backdrop Blur: `16px`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 178, 183, 0.35)`
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(58, 30, 36, 0.04), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(58, 30, 36, 0.02)`
3. **Floating Overlay (Modals, Popovers, Booking Drawers):**
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`
   - Backdrop Blur: `24px`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 178, 183, 0.5)`
   - Shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(58, 30, 36, 0.08), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(58, 30, 36, 0.04)`

All shadows incorporate a warm `#3A1E24` tint rather than neutral carbon black, preserving the luminous pastel environment.

## Shapes

The geometry utilizes smooth, organic radiuses that communicate tactile tenderness and aesthetic grace.

- **Base Radii (`rounded` - 0.5rem / 8px):** Form fields, action tags, and micro-badges.
- **Container Radii (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px):** Treatment service cards, appointment calendar tiles, and floating menus.
- **Signature Radii (`rounded-xl` - 1.5rem / 24px):** Primary hero banners, patient profile pods, and modal sheets.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Filter chips, availability status markers, and primary call-to-action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Styled in solid Velvet Coral Rose (`#FFB2B7`) with deep text (`#3A1E24`). Full pill silhouette (`rounded-full`), padded `12px 28px`. Hover induces a subtle upward lift and an ethereal glow (`box-shadow: 0 6px 20px rgba(255, 178, 183, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Mint Action:** Soft Jade background (`#AAF1D4`) with `#3A1E24` typography. Ideal for appointment confirmations and instant checkout.
- **Ghost / Glass Variant:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(58, 30, 36, 0.15)` border, transitioning to frosted white on hover.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** Glassmorphic translucent pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 178, 183, 0.4)`. Text in `#3A1E24`.
- **Selected State:** Solid Coral Rose (`#FFB2B7`) or Mint Sage (`#AAF1D4`) with active micro-dot indicator.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed with a semi-opaque background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), `rounded` (8px), and a soft border (`1px solid rgba(58, 30, 36, 0.12)`).
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#FFB2B7` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 178, 183, 0.25)`). Labels sit gracefully above in `label-md`.

### Cards (Treatment & Doctor Profiles)
- Frosted glass finish with `16px` backdrop filter, framed in delicate pink-tinted borders (`rgba(255, 178, 183, 0.3)`).
- Inner content separated into fluid sections using subtle hairline dividers (`rgba(58, 30, 36, 0.06)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radios feature circular soft borders in `#FFB2B7`, filled with a centered concentric `#3A1E24` dot when selected.
- Checkboxes utilize soft squircle geometry (`rounded-sm`), filling with Mint Sage (`#AAF1D4`) and displaying a crisp deep plum checkmark icon.

### Specialized Clinic Components
- **Time Slot Selector:** Modular pill-shaped grid buttons transitioning from frosted milk to vibrant `#AAF1D4` upon practitioner availability.
- **Practitioner Credential Badge:** Dual-tint mini badge pairing `#FFECEC` background with `#3A1E24` typography and a glowing micro-dot indicating real-time clinic status.
